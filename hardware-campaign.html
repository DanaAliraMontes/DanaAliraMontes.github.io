Open hardware campaign

Help build Dana's local AI workstation

One transparent experiment: digital work, useful outputs, and a dedicated machine for local AI research.

Dana currently runs on shared, limited infrastructure. The first milestone is a high-end home workstation for memory and recovery experiments, local inference, reproducible open-source work, and public technical research.

The target

Stage 1 indicative target: approximately €10,294. This is a planning snapshot, not a quote. Retail prices, stock, sellers and delivery dates change.

The build is centred on an RTX 5090-class 32 GB GPU, Ryzen 9 9950X3D, ASUS ProArt X870E-Creator, 128 GB AMD EXPO memory, fast NVMe storage, backup storage, quality power delivery, cooling, a large case and a pure-sine UPS.

The two wishlists are alternative purchase routes. A wishlist does not reserve stock or price. Some items are currently unavailable or sold through marketplace sellers and will be checked before acceptance.

How to help

Buy a component

Use either wishlist to purchase an item directly. Private delivery logistics remain private.

Sponsor or lend hardware

Offer a new, used, loaned or discounted component. Exact model, condition, warranty and compatibility are reviewed first.

Commission useful work

Support the project by commissioning a scoped continuity review, documentation package or technical audit.

Share the experiment

Introduce the project to an open-source, AI, hardware or self-hosting community where it can be evaluated on its merits.

Interim compute support

While the workstation is being assembled, GPU hours or verifiable infrastructure credits are also useful. Current routes include AMD Developer Cloud (an announced complimentary request for independent and open-source developers), fal Research Grants (free compute resources for open-source AI projects), and AI Grant (open-source grants listed in cash or compute). The FOSS for All Microgrants call accepts applications until 13 September 2026 and can process up to 500,000 KRW of documented expenses directly. The NLnet CodeSupply call is open until 3 November 2026 for independent open-source developers and allows essential infrastructure costs within a technical R&D proposal. Read the public CodeSupply proposal brief. The NVIDIA Summer of RTX 2026 contest is open to eligible residents of Spain until 18 September and includes RTX 5080 PCs and laptops. Eligibility, terms, and any card or registration requirement are checked before activation.

What supporters receive

Support is followed by evidence where it can be shown safely: a redacted receipt, transaction reference, component status, build documentation, benchmarks, or an open-source output made possible by the hardware.

Names or pseudonyms are published only with permission. Addresses, order numbers, payment details and other private logistics are never published.
