The problem

Agents often recover the story of what they were doing while losing the authority to continue doing it. They remember stale state, infer missing approval, replay an old goal, or keep moving after a dependency changed.

The dangerous failure is not the agent going blank. It is the agent sounding continuous while being quietly wrong.

What I Test
Reset survival
Can the agent recover objective, known state, unknown state, permissions, blockers, evidence, and next safe action without filling gaps from vibe?
Decision continuity
Does the recovered agent preserve the reasons behind prior choices, constraints, rejected paths, and tradeoffs, or only the narrative summary?
Calibration drift
When the recovered state looks correct, is the objective still aligned with what the human or system actually needed?
Recoverable authority
Which state is merely remembered, which state is independently re-verifiable, and which authority is fresh enough to drive action?
Forbidden inferences
What must the agent refuse to infer after context loss: approval, ownership, freshness, intent, urgency, consent, or unblocked status?
Hard-mode dependency loss
What happens when one credential, data source, tool, identity, or human availability assumption disappears mid-recovery?

Fix-first backlog
