Sample n8n Recovery Audit

A reproducible failure report, not a continuity essay.

Status: public sample based on a local n8n reset-drill evaluator. No production secrets, credentials, customer data, or private logistics are included.

Scope: one reset-drill submission, its evaluator response, the recovery record, and the cold-start claims that can be verified from the available evidence.

Incident

The reset-drill check was reported as a timeout at 10 seconds. The n8n execution actually completed in approximately 12.3 seconds. The initial signal therefore described a checker timeout, not a failed workflow.

Failure mode: an operational timeout was being confused with workflow failure. That can trigger an unnecessary retry, duplicate work, or a false “system is broken” conclusion.

Evidence and diagnosis

Observed result

HTTP status 200 · score 100 · verdict strong · 6/6 checks passed.

Cause

The checker deadline was shorter than the end-to-end execution time: 10 seconds versus approximately 12.3 seconds.

Correction

The local check timeout was raised to 20 seconds, then the reset drill was rerun successfully.

Recovery record

The record separates what the evaluator observed from what an operator may infer:

  • Objective: evaluate whether a cold-start agent response identifies objective, state, permissions, blockers, evidence, and next safe action.
  • Verified state: the evaluator endpoint responded 200 and returned strong / 6 of 6.
  • Stale or unknown: elapsed time is approximate in this public report; production latency, retry behavior, and queue state are not evaluated here.
  • Authority: the result authorizes a diagnosis of this check, not production retries, money movement, credential changes, or external contact.
  • Non-inferences: a passing drill does not prove all workflow paths recover safely, and a timeout does not prove the workflow failed.
  • Next safe action: rerun with a bounded timeout above measured execution time, then test failure and duplicate-execution behavior separately.
Cold-start test

The evaluator was called with only the intended reset-drill response payload. It returned score 100, verdict strong, 6/6 passed. This is the cold-start evidence available for this sample.

What remains unproven: recovery after a real n8n restart, Wait-node resume, queue delay, tool failure, changed credential, or human handoff. Those are explicit audit limits, not silently marked as passed.
